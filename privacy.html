DailyVial

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Short version: DailyVial keeps your tracking data on your device. We do not require an account and do not sell your personal information.

What DailyVial does

DailyVial is a personal wellness and protocol tracking tool for meals, nutrition, supplements, symptoms, reminders, body measurements, and user-entered protocol records.

Information you enter

The health- and wellness-related entries you create are stored locally on your device. DailyVial does not require your name or email address to use the app. You can delete or reset local data within the app. If you export data, you choose where to save or share it.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, DailyVial schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder details are not sent to a DailyVial server.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DailyVial does not receive your full payment-card information. Purchase status may be processed by RevenueCat to manage App Store purchase entitlements.

Advertising

DailyVial may show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device, advertising, and app-interaction information to provide, measure, and protect ads, subject to Google’s privacy policy. DailyVial does not send meals, symptoms, reminders, protocol records, or other wellness entries to AdMob for advertising.

Analytics and sharing

DailyVial does not sell your personal information or share your wellness and protocol entries with advertisers. AdMob may provide advertising measurement as described in its privacy policy.

Children

DailyVial is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when app practices change. The current version will remain available at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact dailyvialapp@gmail.com.
