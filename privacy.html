DailyVial privacy

Updated October 21, 2026

This page describes the current DailyVial build in this repository.

Your entries

Peptide and supplement, dose, injection-site, weight, water, meal, mood, workout, and local Circle-preview entries are stored in your browser on your device. This build does not send those entries to a DailyVial account or database.

Reminders and appointment notes

Reminder names, schedules and appointment notes are stored in this browser. Optional browser alerts use generic text without product or dose details. Calendar exports omit product names and depend on your calendar application. The downloaded visit report contains your personal records; store it privately.

AI and cloud services

AI meal-photo estimates, live Nova AI, Google sign-in and cloud sync are disabled in this release and marked coming soon. The app does not load the Firebase SDK or send questions, meal photos or journal entries to Firebase or Gemini. Manual tracking requires no account.

Tour and app guide

Walkthrough offered/completed status is stored in this browser. The live walkthrough uses an in-memory practice copy: practice entries, points and appearance changes are discarded. Uploads, live AI, location searches, account actions and notifications are disabled in practice. Existing saved records are restored on exit; reloading also discards practice changes. Nova offers a written local app guide. Built-in guide questions and replies stay in the tab. Live AI is coming soon and cannot be enabled in this release.

Personal settings and backup

Optional display name, appearance mode, default dose unit, reduced-animation preference, cosmetic unlocks and Stardust balance are saved only in this browser. A downloaded JSON backup includes your personal journal and preferences; store it privately. There is no DailyVial account sync. The iPhone build can restore its Apple purchases; Apple manages subscription billing and cancellation.

Removed site entries

Removing a site entry or resetting a product’s site history keeps a recoverable copy in the browser-local undo history. Dose logs remain unless their removal is explicitly selected. Undo restores removed entries. Clearing this site’s browser storage also removes its undo history; there is no server backup.

Photos and scanning

Meal-photo selection, camera capture and AI scanning are disabled and marked coming soon. Manual nutrition entries are stored locally. Progress-photo previews are separate from meal scanning.

Community and accounts

Circle posts and reactions are local previews and are not shared with other users. Google/Firebase sign-in and cloud sync are disabled in this release. The iPhone build uses Apple for Plus purchases. No advertising network or analytics service is connected.

iPhone subscriptions

The iPhone build offers DailyVial Plus monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions through Apple. Apple processes payment using your Apple Account. DailyVial does not receive your card or bank details. StoreKit verifies subscription transactions on your device; the app uses product identifiers and expiration dates to determine Plus access. Purchase status is refreshed from Apple and is not uploaded with your journal. Restore Purchases asks Apple to sync your transactions. Manage or cancel renewals in your Apple subscription settings. The web build remains a plan preview without checkout.

Plus and exports

On the web, listed prices and the session feature preview are demonstrations and no payment is made. In the iPhone build, prices are loaded from Apple and a confirmed Apple purchase is required for paid access. The web preview also has a local Stardust Plus-day reward. That editable local reward does not authorize paid iPhone features. No advertising provider is connected. Journal exports create a CSV download on your device; you control whether to share it. Do not upload an exported journal to a public repository.

Stardust

Daily check-ins, drop balances, reward redemptions, and optional theme choices are stored in this browser. Stardust is stored locally and does not convert into money or AI scan credits. Local Plus-day rewards do not activate AI features or purchase an Apple subscription.

Optional location and nearby food

Pressing Use my location requests one browser location fix. Rounded coordinates (two decimal places) are sent to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service to find mapped food spots. DailyVial does not continuously track you or store your location in persistent browser storage. Choosing a city sends that public city-center coordinate instead. Search results are cached in page memory and can be cleared. The external map service receives normal network information such as your IP address. Opening menu, map, or nutrition links visits the corresponding external website. About the public map service.

Hosting

When you visit the hosted version, the hosting provider receives normal web requests needed to serve the app. The app's images and interface assets are included in its HTML file.

Clearing local entries

You can clear this site's browser storage. Download a local backup from Profile before clearing storage or uninstalling. Uninstalling the mobile app can also remove its local entries. Clearing browser storage removes local entries; this build has no server copy to restore.
