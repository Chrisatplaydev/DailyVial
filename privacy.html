DailyVial

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Short version: DailyVial is designed to keep your tracking data on your device. We do not require an account and do not sell your personal information.

What DailyVial does

DailyVial is a personal wellness and protocol tracking tool. It lets you record information such as meals, nutrition, supplements, symptoms, reminders, body measurements, and user-entered protocol records.

Information you enter

The information you enter into DailyVial, including health- and wellness-related entries, is stored locally on your device by the app. DailyVial does not require you to create an account, provide your name, or provide an email address to use the app.

You control your data. You can delete entries or reset the app's local data from within the app. If you export data, you choose where to save or share the exported file.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, DailyVial schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder details remain on your device and are not sent to a DailyVial server.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. DailyVial does not receive your full payment-card information. When purchases are enabled, purchase status may be processed by RevenueCat, a service used to manage App Store purchase entitlements. Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy practices apply to their respective services.

Advertising

DailyVial does not currently include an advertising network or third-party behavioral advertising SDK. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Analytics and sharing

DailyVial does not use third-party analytics or sell your personal information. We do not share the wellness and protocol entries you create with advertisers.

Children

DailyVial is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. The current version will always be available at this page, with its effective date shown above.
