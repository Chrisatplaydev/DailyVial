DailyVial privacy

Updated October 14, 2026

This page describes the current DailyVial build in this repository.

Your entries

Medication, dose, weight, water, meal, mood, workout, and local community-preview entries are stored in your browser on your device. This build does not send those entries to a DailyVial account or database.

Photos and scanning

Photos you choose are displayed locally for preview. Automatic image recognition and scanning services are not connected in this build.

Community and accounts

The community is a local design preview. Posts and reactions are not shared with other people. There is no connected account service, payment service, advertising network, or analytics service in this build.

Hosting

When you visit the hosted version, the hosting provider receives normal web requests needed to serve the app. The app's images and interface assets are included in its HTML file.

Clearing local entries

You can use Profile → Reset demo data in the app, or clear this site's browser storage. Clearing browser storage removes local entries; this build has no server copy to restore.
