# Live app walkthrough

Replaces the introductory slide deck with a coach over the actual interface. First-time users can accept or skip one short invitation. Replay from Profile → Live app walkthrough.

Five chapter buttons cover Home, Peptides, Progress, Nourish and Circle. A spotlight identifies the real control. Clicking highlighted shortcuts advances the coach; users can also choose Next, Back, any chapter or Exit. Escape exits.

Water, weight, meal and small-win forms can be submitted in a protected practice copy. Dose and reminder forms can be inspected without creating records. Practice points and appearance changes do not persist. Real saved data is restored on exit; a reload discards practice work. Latest saved data from another tab takes precedence when restoring.

The walkthrough blocks file selection, live AI, location searches, notification permission, account actions, billing and downloads. It does not prescribe doses or recommend needle placement. Subscription checkout, cloud journal sync, live AI and a connected community are still deployment work.

Validation: standalone JavaScript compilation; practice-state isolation and restoration tests; browser checks of click-driven navigation, water and post submissions, chapter jumps, reload restoration and phone-sized layout.
