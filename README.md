# DailyVial

[Open DailyVial](https://chrisatplaydev.github.io/DailyVial/DailyVial.html)

A galaxy-themed peptide/supplement journal with local dose and injection-site records, food and water logs, progress entries, reminders, meal inspiration, Stardust cosmetics and a hands-on walkthrough.

## Connected AI pilot

Nova and meal-photo estimates use an authenticated Firebase/Gemini backend. AI is restricted to the verified owner account during testing. The server enforces allowances; the Gemini secret is never included in the website. Google sign-in does not upload the journal or activate a paid subscription. [Pilot access and limits](AI-PILOT.md).

## Current limits

Journals and Circle posts remain local to the browser. Billing, ads, shared community, cloud journal sync and closed-app push are not connected. Plus prices and session previews are prototypes; no purchase occurs. Photo calorie results are estimates requiring review. Nothing prescribes doses or needle placement.
